StamCont does **not** currently require contributors to sign a separate Contributor License Agreement.

Unless a contribution explicitly states otherwise, contributions intentionally submitted for inclusion in StamCont are accepted under the contribution terms of the Apache License 2.0, including Section 5 ("Submission of Contributions").

By submitting a pull request or other contribution, you represent that you have the right to submit the work under those terms.

The Contributor License Agreement previously stored at this path belonged to the upstream Continue project and named Continue Dev, Inc. It is not presented as the contribution agreement for StamCont.
