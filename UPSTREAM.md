## History policy

The standalone StamCont repository intentionally represents the inherited Continue source as a single provenance/import root rather than retaining the full upstream commit graph on its default branch.

This history policy does **not** claim authorship of the imported source. The imported source remains attributable to its original authors and copyright holders. The original upstream history is available from the Continue repository.

StamCont-specific commits represent subsequent work performed on this imported baseline.
