# StamCont Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in StamCont a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity, or sexual orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive project environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive technical criticism;
- focusing disagreements on ideas, evidence, and code;
- showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source project.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, documentation, or other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in StamCont project spaces and when an individual is representing the StamCont project or community.

## Enforcement

Do not send StamCont conduct reports to Continue Dev addresses.

For a confidential conduct report, contact the StamCont repository maintainer through a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private channel is available, open a minimal issue requesting private contact **without including sensitive incident details publicly**.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible and reviewed in the context of the available evidence and project scope.
